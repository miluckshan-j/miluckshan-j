## What drives me

- **I'm a builder.** Nothing beats taking an idea and turning it into something people can actually use.
- **I care how things feel.** I have an eye for good UI/UX, and I'd rather get the details right than ship something that merely works.
- **I notice things.** Small details in an interface, an edge case nobody mentioned, the pattern behind a recurring bug. Noticing early saves a lot of time later.
- **I'm curious and I like solving problems.** I keep digging until I understand the problem, and then until it's solved.
- **I like startups.** I've worked in them, and I want to build one of my own.
- **I like a good story.** I enjoy explaining technical ideas as stories.
